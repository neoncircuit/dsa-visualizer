Key Ideas

                    
    
                        
  • O(1) = instant, O(log n) = fast, O(n) = once each, O(n^2) = slow
    • 
                        
  • "In-place" = no extra list needed (space O(1))
    • 
                        
  • "Stable" = equal items keep their original order
    • 
                        
  • Small lists (<20): Insertion Sort is fastest
    • 
                        
  • Large lists: Merge Sort or Quick Sort
    • 
                        
  • Binary Search needs sorted data but is much faster
    •
